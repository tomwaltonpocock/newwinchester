Generate a QR once the final domain is known.

Recommended:
- Encode the campaign URL: https://<your-domain>/chronicle
  (the /chronicle redirect tags responses as source=chronicle for the admin Source distribution)
- Use a high error-correction level (Q or H) so a small VW logo can sit centre.
- Test by scanning on:
  - iPhone Safari and Camera
  - Android Chrome
  - Samsung Internet
  - Desktop webcam if relevant
- Include the URL printed beneath the QR for sighted readers.
- Tools that work: https://www.qr-code-generator.com, https://qrcode.tec-it.com,
  or any local CLI (`qrencode -o vfw.png 'https://...'`).

Save the resulting image as /public/qr.png and reference it from print collateral.
